VIJAYADASHAMI SIGNIFICANCE

The Sacred Victory of Goodness

Vijayadashami marks the triumph of Lord Rama over Ravana, symbolizing the eternal victory of Dharma over Adharma, light over darkness, and truth over falsehood.

It also honors Goddess Durga's conquest over the demon Mahishasura after nine nights of devotion. On this revered day, families gather to exchange blessings, perform Shami Puja, light diyas, and welcome prosperity into every home.

“May the divine light of Vijayadashami illuminate your path with wisdom, joy, and endless peace.”
