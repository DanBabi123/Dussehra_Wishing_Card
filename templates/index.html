VIJAYADASHAMI ESSENCE

The Sacred Victory of Dharma

Dussehra, also known as Vijayadashami, marks the glorious victory of Lord Rama over the demon king Ravana, symbolizing the eternal triumph of righteousness (Dharma) over evil (Adharma).

It also celebrates Goddess Durga's victory over the buffalo demon Mahishasura after nine days of fierce battle. On this auspicious day, people burn effigies of Ravana, perform Shami Puja, exchange Aapta leaves as symbolic gold, and seek blessings for new beginnings, prosperity, and wisdom.

“May the divine blessings of Lord Rama fill your life with strength, courage, and radiant joy on this Vijayadashami.”
